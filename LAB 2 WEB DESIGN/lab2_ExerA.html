About the Event

Join us for an intensive one-day hackathon where students collaborate to solve real-world problems through software engineering.

Keynote Speaker: Dr. Alya Mawaddah

Dr. Alya Mawaddah is an AI researcher who will share insights on leveraging artificial intelligence in modern web applications.

Activity Highlight: 3-Hour Rapid Prototyping Sprint

Participants will team up to design and pitch a functional prototype within a strict three-hour window.
