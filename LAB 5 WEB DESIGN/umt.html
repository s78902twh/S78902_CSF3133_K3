About the University

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a university in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially chartered on 1 February 2007. The university began as the Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in 1979.

The facility was established to provide students of the Fisheries and Marine Science programs at UPM with practical training. Later, it was upgraded to a branch campus and underwent several name changes before becoming an independent university focused on marine sciences, maritime studies, and oceanography.
